# Voice Line — Small questions. Keep moving.

## 00:00 — Questions throughout the day

A quick question should not become a new task. Between emails, meetings, and ideas, ask it while it is fresh.

## 00:09 — A new job for Copilot

Voice Line puts a voice shortcut on your Windows desktop. That unused Copilot key? Give it a new job.

## 00:18 — Hold. Speak. Release. Listen.

Hold the key, and speak your question. Release it. Voice Line turns your speech into text. Hear the reply, read along, or press again to interrupt.

## 00:32 — Quick questions and follow-ups

Ask what to focus on next. Then turn the answer into three simple steps. Follow up in the same conversation. No need to repeat the whole story.

## 00:44 — Use the plan you already have

Sign in through Codex with your existing eligible ChatGPT plan. In local mode, there is no separate speech API bill. Normal plan limits still apply.

## 00:58 — Local voice. Online answers.

Your microphone audio becomes text locally, using Whisper. Codex answers online. Local mode does not mean offline answers. Piper reads the reply on your computer.

## 01:14 — Type a quick rewrite

Prefer to type? Tap Copilot and write a question. For example, rewrite a delivery update. Typed answers stay silent.

## 01:24 — Ask about what you see

Capture part of your screen, paste an image, or attach an image file. Ask what the chart means: revenue rose sixty percent.

## 01:34 — Get code. Copy it.

Need a small bit of code? Ask a specific question. Copy the code block, and put it to work.

## 01:44 — Turn an idea into an image

Describe an image you want to create, with an image command. Review the result. Image generation uses your plan allowance too.

## 01:54 — Return to it. Save it.

Reopen the previous conversation, or return to its history. Export useful conversations as Markdown, with their images.
